Where does this piece go?

Photograph the box art, then photograph a piece. The finder searches the whole picture and tells you the row, the column and which way to turn it.

Open the finder → Works in the browser on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.
  1. 1

    Key

    Photograph the finished picture on the box lid and crop it to the edges. Enter the piece count.

  2. 2

    Piece

    Lay a piece on plain paper and photograph it. The app cuts it out from the background for you.

  3. 3

    Place

    See the spot marked on the key, a close-up, and the piece turned the way it sits. Runner-up spots too.

How it works

Everything runs on your device with plain canvas pixels. There is no server, no account and no model download.

  • Cut-out. The background colour is read from the photo's frame, the piece is separated with an Otsu threshold, holes are filled and the bevelled rim is shaved off.
  • Straighten. The straight sides of a piece dominate its outline's edge directions, so the peak of that histogram gives the tilt to undo.
  • Search. The piece is slid over a small copy of the key at every position, in four turns and three sizes, scored by zero-mean colour correlation with a penalty for colour drift.
  • Refine. The strongest peaks are re-scored on a finer copy of the key, then the top three distinct spots are shown with a confidence verdict.
Tips: hold the phone flat above the box to avoid skew, avoid glare, and photograph pieces on paper that contrasts with their colours. Pieces of plain sky or water will match many places, which the app tells you.
